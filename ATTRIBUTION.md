## Algorithmic Foundations
1. **Zhang-Suen Thinning Algorithm:**
   - Classical 8-connected morphological skeletonization algorithm for binary images.
   - Reference: T. Y. Zhang and C. Y. Suen, "A fast parallel algorithm for thinning digital patterns," Communications of the ACM, vol. 27, no. 3, pp. 236–239, 1984.
   - Implemented from scratch in pure NumPy vector operations without proprietary code.

2. **Otsu Bimodal Thresholding:**
   - Reference: Nobuyuki Otsu, "A threshold selection method from gray-level histograms," IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, vol. 9, no. 1, pp. 62–66, 1979.
   - Implemented from scratch in pure NumPy.

3. **Douglas-Peucker Polyline Simplification:**
   - Reference: David Douglas and Thomas Peucker, "Algorithms for the reduction of the number of points required to represent a digitized line or its caricature," Cartographica, 1973.
   - Implemented independently in Python.
